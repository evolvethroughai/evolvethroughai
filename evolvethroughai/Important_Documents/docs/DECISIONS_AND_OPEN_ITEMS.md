- Client logos

- Performance benchmarks

- Course durations
- Course prices
- Completed project repositories

Avoid generic layouts and repetitive cards.

Judge the result visually.

### Rule 6 — Meaningful Interactions

Animations should explain, guide, or improve the experience.

Do not add motion solely for decoration.

### Rule 7 — Build Incrementally

Do not build the entire website without intermediate review.

### Rule 8 — Protect Existing Work

Inspect before modifying.

Preserve original assets.

Avoid destructive Git operations.

Do not delete source material without approval.

### Rule 9 — Beginner-Friendly Communication

I want clear, practical explanations.

Avoid overwhelming me with excessive technical details when one decision is needed.

Explain unfamiliar tools in simple language.

### Rule 10 — One Actionable Step at a Time

When guiding me through setup, deployment, or external tools, give one concrete action and wait for my confirmation when appropriate.

### Rule 11 — Verify Before Claiming Completion

Report what was actually tested.

Distinguish implementation from verification.

### Rule 12 — Keep Documentation Updated

When an important decision changes, update the relevant project document.

Do not allow outdated decisions to remain presented as current.

### Rule 13 — Separate Current and Future Offerings

Clearly distinguish launched, in-development, planned, and exploratory business capabilities.

### Rule 14 — Respect My Time

Avoid unnecessary tool switching, redundant rewrites, endless planning, and premature complexity.

### Rule 15 — Seek Approval at Meaningful Milestones

Obtain approval for major visual direction changes, destructive changes, external integrations, deployments, and new business commitments.
