## PART 8 — DEVOPS TO AI ENGINEERING PROGRESSION

One of the central concepts of Evolve Through AI is that modern DevOps and cloud engineering can provide a strong foundation for AI-enabled engineering.

The career progression concept is:

**Engineering Foundations → DevOps and Cloud → Platform Engineering → AI-Assisted Engineering → AI / Agentic Engineering**

The website should communicate this progression through engaging visual storytelling.

Explain that skills such as automation, cloud infrastructure, CI/CD, containers, Kubernetes, observability, security, and production operations remain valuable when building and operating AI applications.

Also explain that AI engineering requires additional skills.

These may include:

- Python
- APIs
- LLM fundamentals
- AI application development
- Retrieval-augmented generation
- Vector databases
- Evaluation
- Agent orchestration
- Model Context Protocol
- AI security and safety
- Deployment and monitoring of AI applications

Do not imply that learning DevOps automatically makes someone an AI engineer.

This progression should be educational, inspiring, and technically responsible.
