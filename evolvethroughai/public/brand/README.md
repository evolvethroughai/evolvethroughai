# Evolve Through AI brand artwork — candidate 01

Prepared 2026-10-26. OWNER APPROVED for implementation on 2026-10-26. Integrated locally; not deployed.

Independent vector reconstruction of FINAL_SELECTED_BRAND_BOARD.png and AI_CIRCUIT_CONCEPT_REFERENCE.png, including the curved AI circuit trace and round terminal node. Previous no-circuit logo kit was not used as the final artwork. This is a visual interpretation of raster references, not an extracted original vector.

## Files

- logo-light / logo-dark: transparent horizontal lockups for light / dark backgrounds.
- logo-compact-light / dark: single-line alternative.
- logo-signature-light / dark: descriptor and tagline for large-format use.
- symbol: full-color standalone mark. symbol-small: simplified mark for tiny contexts.
- logo-mono-dark / light and symbol-mono-dark / light: single-color marks; circuit appears as transparent negative space.
- icon-dark / light: opaque application tiles. PNGs include 512px, 192px and 180px Apple touch icon.
- favicon.svg and favicon.ico: simplified trace and node. ICO includes 16, 32, 48 and 64px PNG representations.

All SVG lettering is outlined; no fonts, embedded bitmaps, scripts or external resources are required. PNGs were rasterized directly from SVG using resvg. SVG masters are the editable artwork source. Preserve aspect ratio. Avoid the descriptor in small headers. Use the simplified favicon at tiny sizes. Allow clear space around the mark; do not crop, recolor gradients or add glow.

Palette: midnight #0B1220, blue #2563EB, cyan #06B6D4, violet #8B5CF6, off-white #F8FAFC. Wordmark: Inter Bold; signature: Inter Regular, outlined under SIL Open Font License (included).

Approval previews remain at ../../design-review/brand-approval/preview.html as a historical review artifact. Header, footer, icons and metadata now use this identity. Social preview is 1200 × 630px. Configure absolute production social-image URLs once the production origin is confirmed.
